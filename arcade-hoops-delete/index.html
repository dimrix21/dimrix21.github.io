Delete your data — Arcade Hoops

Arcade Hoops ("Basketball Arcade Machines 3D") · Developer: Hezi Dimri · Contact: dimrihezi@gmail.com

What can be deleted

From version 1.1 the game keeps a small record for the world ranking: a random user ID, your player name, and your best score on each machine (with the ball and mastery levels shown next to it). Everything else (tickets, balls, machines, settings) stays on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the game. Details are in the privacy policy.

In the game (immediate)

  1. Open the game and tap PROFILE (top left).
  2. Tap DELETE MY ONLINE DATA, then DELETE.

Your player name, your scores on every ranking and your user ID are deleted at once. Your progress on the phone stays.

Without the game

Email dimrihezi@gmail.com with the subject "Arcade Hoops data deletion" and your player name as it appears on the ranking. We delete the record, and every score under it, within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is kept

Nothing linked to you. Anonymous usage statistics (Firebase Analytics, not linked to the ranking record) expire on their own after at most 14 months.
