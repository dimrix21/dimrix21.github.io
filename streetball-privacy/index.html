Privacy Policy — Streetball

Effective date: October 26, 2026 · Developer: Hezi Dimri · Contact: dimrihezi@gmail.com

App covered by this policy Streetball — Android package com.hezi.streetball

1. Overview

Streetball is an arcade basketball game played offline on your device. We do not collect, store or share any personal information. This page explains what the game keeps and where.

2. What stays on your device

  • Game progress. Your scores, best scores, tickets, unlocked machines and balls, boosters, mastery progress and the daily-gift streak are saved in a file on your device only.
  • Settings. Sound, music and vibration preferences are saved on your device only.

None of this leaves your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes it completely.

3. What we do not do

  • The game does not use the internet and asks for no network permission.
  • No account, sign-in, email or name is ever requested.
  • No advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.
  • No access to your photos, camera, microphone, location or contacts.
  • The only permission the game uses is vibration, for haptic feedback.

4. Purchases

Tickets, machines, balls and boosters are earned and spent inside the game. There are no real-money purchases in the current version. If that changes, this policy will be updated before the change is released.

5. Children

Streetball is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no information at all, it collects none from children either.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: dimrihezi@gmail.com

This policy applies only to the Streetball app.
